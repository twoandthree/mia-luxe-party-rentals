About Us

Proudly serving Tucson, Arizona, Mia Luxe Party Rentals brings style, fun, and stress-free setup to celebrations of every kind. Whether you're hosting a backyard birthday or a family get-together, we provide essential tables and chairs alongside interactive favorites—including our signature slime carts, Giant Jenga, Giant Connect 4, Giant Ping Pong, and Cornhole—so you can focus on making memories with the people who matter most.

Our Story

Mia Luxe Party Rentals was created in honor of my daughter, Mia, whose joy, creativity, and personality inspired the heart behind this business. What started as a dream became something meaningful: a family-inspired business built around celebrating life's special moments, creating memories, and bringing people together. Named in honor of Mia, this business represents love, family, creativity, and the belief that every celebration deserves a little extra magic. From party rentals and outdoor games to our creative slime experiences, our goal is to bring fun, style, and a special touch to every event. Proudly serving Tucson, Arizona, Mia Luxe Party Rentals is more than just a rental business. It's a dream built with love, inspired by family, and created to make celebrations unforgettable.
